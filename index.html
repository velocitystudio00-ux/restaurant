Behind the Scenes

Our Kitchen Philosophy

Every ingredient used at Ember & Spice undergoes rigorous selection. Our Head Chef brings over 18 years of international culinary experience, blending centuries-old Indian royal recipes with French cooking techniques.

We believe in sustainability, zero food waste, and supporting local farmers across Maharashtra. Every sauce is simmered from scratch, and every bread is hand-stretched to order.
